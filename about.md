# ForcePlatformerButtonsInClassic
A few Classic levels, like [Pirdal Platformy](level:133757547), rely on L/R controls for their gameplay gimmicks. Gameplay gimmicks that, up until now, would exclude mobile players.

Well, not anymore!

Mod suggested by [el_don_look_2](https://discord.com/users/1115779678905581568).
